Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

To be completed by the operator. The identity of the operator still needs to be filled in below, in the “Who we are” section. This document is a plain-language draft describing what the site actually does; have it reviewed before relying on it.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the website at sweet-bonanza.stream (“the site”). The site is operated by [operator name and, if applicable, registration details].

2. The short version

We do not ask you for any personal information, and we do not collect any. There are no accounts, no registration, no sign-in, no payments and no newsletters. We set no cookies and run no analytics or advertising scripts.

3. What is stored on your device

The site keeps two small pieces of information in your browser’s local storage, so that it behaves sensibly when you come back:

  • Your language choice — the interface language you picked.
  • Recently opened games — a short list of game names, so the site can show you what you looked at last.

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us. It is not linked to you as a person and cannot identify you. You can erase it at any time by clearing site data for this website in your browser settings.

4. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub’s infrastructure processes the technical details of each request — including your IP address, the time of the request and your browser’s user agent — in order to deliver the pages to you and to protect the service from abuse. That processing is governed by GitHub’s own privacy statement, which you can read at docs.github.com. We have no access to those logs.

5. Third-party content

The games on this site are not hosted by us. They are publicly available demonstration builds, loaded directly from the servers of their developer (Pragmatic Play) inside an embedded frame. The cover images shown in the game list are loaded from the same developer’s content delivery network.

This has a consequence worth stating plainly: when you open a game, or even just load the game list, your browser connects directly to those third-party servers. Those servers can see your IP address and the technical details of the request, and the game itself may store data in your browser in order to work. This happens under the third party’s own terms and privacy policy, not ours. We do not receive, see or control any of it, and we cannot change how the embedded game behaves.

6. Children

The site is not intended for children. It is meant for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect information about anyone, of any age.

7. Your rights

Data-protection law gives you rights to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side for us to hand over, correct or erase. The information stored locally in your browser is under your own control and can be cleared as described in section 3. For the technical logs described in section 4, and for anything the third-party game servers process, those rights are exercised with GitHub and with the game developer respectively.

8. Changes to this policy

If what the site does changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@sweet-bonanza.stream.
